EXPERIMENT 010 · LEARNING NOTES

Can a CV become a playable learning adventure?

I wanted to explore whether a professional story could become something people actively discover, rather than another list of roles and achievements. Career Quest turns four chapters of my experience into a small world, then asks the player to practise a coaching decision inside the coffee house.

THE QUESTION

Could exploration add meaning?

Would movement, narrative and choice help someone understand a career more actively than reading a traditional CV?

WHAT I BUILT

A story you can enter

A pixel-art journey through four career chapters, with guides, conversations and a five-step coaching mission.

THE LEARNING

Practise, not just read

The coffee-house chapter asks the player to listen, identify a need, choose focused support, give feedback and decide how to review the outcome.

WHAT I WAS TESTING

Purposeful game mechanics

Could the mechanics create curiosity while also carrying the learning, rather than functioning as decoration around the content?

The experiment was not simply whether I could turn a CV into a game. It was whether exploration, narrative and decision-making could make professional experience useful and meaningful to another person.

What I learned: AI-assisted development can make an interactive prototype possible very quickly. The harder and more important work is deciding what every interaction teaches. This remains an exploratory prototype, not evidence that gamification automatically improves learning. The next step would be testing it with real users for clarity, engagement and learning transfer.
